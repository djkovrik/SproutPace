## Positioning

SproutPace is an offline-first home microgreens planner for several concurrent trays. Its wedge is a feasible repeated-harvest plan that respects light/dark place capacity and adapts to manually confirmed real stages. It is not a generic gardening app, a plant identifier, a hydroponics controller, or a commercial farm system.
